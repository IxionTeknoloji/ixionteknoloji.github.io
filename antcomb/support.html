AntComb Support

Questions or problems? Email info@theixion.com. Tell us your device, your iOS version and what happened. Please do not send playlist passwords.

Does AntComb include channels or movies?

No. AntComb is a player and includes no content. It plays only the M3U playlists and Xtream Codes accounts you add. We cannot help you find or buy a playlist.

How do I add a playlist?

Tap Add Playlist on the first screen, or go to Settings, then Playlists. Choose M3U URL or Xtream Codes and enter the details from your provider.

A video will not play or keeps stopping

Check that the same playlist works elsewhere, since streams come directly from your provider. Some providers limit connections. If yours does, lower Download connections in Settings, then App Settings.

Why is my download not starting?

Downloads run one at a time, oldest first, and Download on Wi-Fi only is on by default. A waiting download shows why it is waiting. Tap its download button to move it to the front.

Can downloads continue in the background?

Yes, with Allow downloads while app is in background in Settings, then App Settings. iOS decides how fast they run and may slow them on low battery or in Low Power Mode.

How do I remove my data?

Swipe left on a playlist or a download to delete it. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.
