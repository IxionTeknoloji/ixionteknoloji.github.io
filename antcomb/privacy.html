AntComb Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

Who we are

AntComb is a video player made by Ixion Teknoloji. It provides no channels, movies or series. It plays only the playlists and accounts you add.

What stays on your device

Playlist addresses, account details, favorites, watch history, settings and downloaded videos are stored only on your device. Passwords are kept in the iOS Keychain. Ixion Teknoloji runs no server for AntComb and receives none of this.

Your video sources

When you add a playlist or account, AntComb connects directly to the addresses you entered to fetch the catalogue, the guide, pictures and video. Those services see your IP address and what you request, under their own terms and privacy policies.

Advertising

AntComb shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To deliver and measure them, Google may collect device identifiers, your approximate location derived from your IP address, the ads you have seen, how you interact with them, and diagnostic and performance data. AntComb does not ask for permission to track you and does not use your advertising identifier. Where the law requires your consent, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked before any ad is requested, and you can change your answer at any time under Settings, AntComb, Privacy Choices. Some US state privacy laws treat this collection as a sale or sharing of personal information. Where you have the right, you can opt out, also under Privacy Choices. Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

What we do not do

AntComb contains no analytics of its own. Ixion Teknoloji sees only summary advertising reports from Google and does not itself receive your personal data.

Keeping and deleting your data

Removing a playlist or a download deletes it from your device. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. Data collected by Google is kept and deleted under Google's policy.

Children

AntComb is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact

We will publish any change to this policy here. For questions, email info@theixion.com.
